PRISMA-P
Rationale: Soil moisture (SM) governs crop water consumption; however, satellite data and point measurements leave a gap at the field scale. While the use of UAVs for SM monitoring is increasing, existing reviews focus either on UAVs in agriculture generally or on satellite-based SM—rather than the acquisition, processing, and reporting of UAV campaigns specifically for SM. This review surveys methods from 2019–2026 and assesses the replicability of the flight operations described in the literature.

Objectives: Systematically identify and summarize primary research from 2019–2026 regarding the estimation/monitoring of soil moisture (SM) via remote sensing in agriculture, with an emphasis on methods applicable using UAVs.
Research questions: which platforms and sensors were used (including combinations); what were the flight and data processing protocols, and what results were reported; which inputs, applications, and methods fall within the priority subset; and which items from the checklist (25 items + SM module) are missing?

Inclusion rules: Topic, methods and setting as defined by the search groups. Primary research (journal article, conference paper, or book chapter kept at audit). Years and language as set in Search.
